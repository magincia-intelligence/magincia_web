## Top of the brief

- **Clare addresses the ALP National Conference on Labor's education reform mandate, linking the Universities Accord Bill to the Hawke and Keating-era university expansion and to the Albanese Government's 2022 commitment to "opening the doors of opportunity" (24 July).** Clare told Labor delegates that educational opportunity is central to Labor's mission: "It's education that does this more than almost anything else. It opens doors. It changes lives. It changes countries." The address — titled "Opening the Doors of Opportunity," the full name of the Universities Accord Bill — reaffirmed the political mandate underpinning the Accord reform suite as the bill continues through parliament. ([Clare, 24 July](https://ministers.education.gov.au/clare/opening-doors-opportunity-address-alp-national-conference))

## International education

- **Department of Education publishes provider-level indicative 2027 international student commencement allocations, giving institutions the first concrete view of how the 295,000 NPL translates to their individual numbers under the managed growth framework (24 July).** The document is the operational companion to Clare's 3 July announcement holding the NPL steady and precedes ATEC's mission-based compact negotiations with eligible higher education providers. ([Department of Education, 24 July](https://www.education.gov.au/managed-system-international-education/resources/indicative-allocations-higher-education-new-overseas-students-commencements-2027))

## Sector data

- **Standing figures:** 2026 NPL 295,000 (+25,000 on 2025); visa refusal rates 69% Nepal, 42% India (early 2026); domestic commencements 413,133 (+4.3% YoY); $50m over-enrolment fund; $50m Structural Adjustment Fund.
