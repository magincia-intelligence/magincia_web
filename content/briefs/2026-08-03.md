## Top of the brief
- **Trump administration eyes a $100,000 fee on Optional Practical Training, the post-graduation work program used by 295,000 international students in 2024/25; Indian nationals make up 49% of participants (31 July).** DHS: "Our immigration system is being reformed to serve American citizens and American workers...not to rapidly import foreigners who take American jobs." The proposal has not been formally enacted but represents a further escalation in US post-study work barriers following the finalised Duration of Status changes; India is Australia's second-largest source market, but a 42% India visa refusal rate (early 2026) and $2,500 student visa fee constrain Australia's capacity to absorb redirected demand. ([The PIE, 31 July](https://thepienews.com/trump-eyes-100000-opt-fee/))
