## Top of the brief

- **Clare releases 2026 NAPLAN results showing numeracy improvements in Years 5, 7, and 9 — up 6.1, 9.5, and 6.2 points respectively since 2023 — but a dip in Year 3 prompting early-years curriculum reform; First Nations Year 7 students in very remote communities improved by 17 points, the strongest finding in that cohort (5 August).** Schools applying phonics instruction and small-group tutoring are producing the sharpest literacy gains: Highvale Primary School (Victoria) lifted the proportion of Year 3 students exceeding in reading by 25.1 percentage points since 2023. NSW and South Australia are now implementing Year 1 numeracy checks across all public schools, with other jurisdictions piloting; revised K–2 mathematics curriculum is to be presented to education ministers in October. Clare: "The reading wars are over. This year's results show that in schools using explicit teaching methods, including phonics, and small-group tutoring to give kids who are struggling extra intensive help, results are going up and up." ([Clare, 5 August](https://jasonclare.com.au/media/naplan-results-show-real-improvement-in-numeracy-but-more-work-to-do/))

- **Panellists at The PIE Live Asia Pacific 2026 urge Australian universities to reframe Pakistan as a long-term strategic market rather than a visa-risk category, citing a population of 260 million — 59% under 30 — and approximately 114,000 annual outbound students, set against a 37% offshore higher education visa refusal rate in January–March 2026 (5 August).** Edify Group CEO Muhammad Azeem Qasim: "Australia has to stop seasonal recruitment and start building long-term relationships." D'Educationist CEO Syed Danish Ali Zaidi: "Two hundred and sixty million people cannot be labelled as high risk." MA Consultants CEO Asad Malik noted that Australia's academic reputation remains strong but competitive attractiveness has eroded through inconsistent financial documentation requirements across institutions and the AUD $2,500 application fee. ([The PIE, 5 August](https://thepienews.com/australian-universities-urged-to-rethink-engagement-with-pakistan/))
