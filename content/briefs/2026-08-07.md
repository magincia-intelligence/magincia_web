## Top of the brief

- **Clare names Professor Mark Rose AM as inaugural First Nations Commissioner of ATEC for a five-year term commencing 17 August — the first person to hold the role as the commission stands up its governance structure (6 August).** Rose brings more than 40 years of Indigenous education experience across higher education, VET, policy, and community advocacy, and was previously Deputy Vice-Chancellor (Indigenous Strategy and Innovation) at Deakin University. ATEC Chief Commissioner Barney Glover: "Professor Rose is an outstanding leader whose deep knowledge of education, policy and community engagement will be tremendous." Clare: "Professor Mark Rose is one of Australia's most respected Indigenous educators and leaders." First Nations university student numbers have risen nearly 13% over the past three years; Commonwealth Supported Places remain uncapped for First Nations students. Universities Australia welcomed the appointment. ([Clare, 6 August](https://jasonclare.com.au/media/mark-rose-am-appointed-atec-first-nations-commissioner/); [UA, 6 August](https://universitiesaustralia.edu.au/media-item/universities-australia-congratulates-atec-first-nations-commissioner/))

## Regulator

- **The Higher Education GBV Regulator publishes a Statement of Regulatory Expectations on providers' use of confidentiality clauses and non-disclosure agreements in gender-based violence disclosures and complaints (5 August).** The statement identifies opportunities to strengthen confidentiality practices and supports providers in assessing whether existing policies and procedures are consistent with regulatory requirements; it follows the EOI for GBV Equity and Response Uplift grants opened in July. ([Department of Education — GBV Regulator, 5 August](https://www.education.gov.au/highereducationgbvregulator/resources/statement-regulatory-expectations))
