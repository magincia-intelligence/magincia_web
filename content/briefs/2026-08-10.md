## Top of the brief

- **Clare launches the *Let's Stop Bullying* campaign — the first targeted federal government advertising campaign on school bullying — backed by $10 million including $5 million for national advertising, with all Australian schools required by Term 1 2027 to publish anti-bullying policies aligned with the National Framework and respond to complaints within two school days (9 August).** One in four students in Years 4–9 experience regular bullying; cyberbullying reports to the eSafety Commissioner rose 455% between 2019 and 2024. The campaign features school-aged children asking viewers to identify who is being bullied, and directs resources to LetsStopBullying.gov.au for students, parents, carers, and school communities. Clare: "we need to nip it in the bud and to act early." ([Clare, 9 August](https://jasonclare.com.au/media/bullying-is-harder-to-spot-than-you-think-new-national-campaign-to-stop-school-bullying/))
