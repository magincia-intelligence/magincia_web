## Top of the brief

- **Clare marks the ARC's 25th anniversary at Shine Dome, Canberra, reaffirming the government's legislative changes to prevent ministerial interference in research grant decisions and tracing four ARC-funded research threads — machine learning at Monash (2002), mobile networks at the University of Sydney (2002), early app development at UNSW, and quantum computing — that together demonstrate the long-horizon rationale for the $1 billion National Competitive Grants Program (13 August).** Clare: "Politicians should not decide which physicist, historian or mathematician has written the better research proposal." The address also reaffirmed the Explore scheme launching March 2027 under the NCGP reforms announced in July. ([Clare, 13 August](https://jasonclare.com.au/media/arc-25-year-anniversary-address/))

## International education

- **Oxford International's Student Global Mobility Index — drawn from 17,925 Indian respondents — finds 81% plan to return to India after studying overseas and 88% would be the first in their family to study abroad; Indian overseas enrolments fell 5.7% in 2025 and interest is diversifying toward European and Asian alternatives (13 August).** Self-motivated decision-making rose sharply from 29% (2023/24) to 49% (2025/26) while family influence fell from 59% to 36%; 70% pursue postgraduate study with business/management at 75% of preferences, and only 6% cite fees as a concern. Australia's 42% visa refusal rate for Indian applicants and $2,500 student visa charge sit as structural barriers to capturing this market as it diversifies away from traditional English-speaking destinations. ([The PIE, 13 August](https://thepienews.com/four-in-five-indian-study-abroad-aspirants-plan-to-return-home/))
