## Top of the brief

- **Clare and Walsh announce $38 million to co-locate early childhood education with primary schools in Western Australia — the first partnership between the Australian and WA governments to deliver 0–5 education on public school sites — with four early learning centres to be built including a Derby facility backed jointly by the federal government ($9 million), the Cook Government ($7.5 million), and the Minderoo Foundation, creating more than 140 places across the state (24 August).** The investment draws on the Building Early Education Fund; co-location at Maarakool Primary School and Greenmount Primary School is designed to reduce double drop-offs and ease school transitions. Clare: "Where you grow up shouldn't determine whether you can access early education." ([Clare, 24 August](https://jasonclare.com.au/media/expanding-access-to-early-education-in-western-australia/))

## International education

- **An analysis of India's international branch campuses finds 13 universities — including five Australian institutions — operating 14 campuses for 2026/27 with an estimated intake capacity of 2,730 students, but actual enrolments expected to fall below half that figure; undergraduate fees average INR 13.3 lakh (115% higher than leading Indian private universities) and postgraduate INR 17.2 lakh, representing approximately 36% of the overseas parent campus fee (24 August).** Programs across 91 offerings — 53 undergraduate and 38 postgraduate — are concentrated in business and STEM; demand is cautious with prospective families taking a "wait-and-watch" approach pending evidence of faculty quality and graduate placement outcomes: "These campuses have a very short runway to prove they can deliver on the outcomes they've promised for a premium fee." ([The PIE, 24 August](https://thepienews.com/small-cohorts-and-premium-fees-put-indian-ibcs-to-the-test/))
