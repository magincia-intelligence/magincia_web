## Top of the brief
- **The Albanese government is reported to be preparing a significant immigration overhaul targeting international students, with proposed measures including onshore visa application bans, restrictions on student dependants and partners, stricter course-change rules, and blocking VET pathway switching ("visa hopping"), with an announcement potentially in September (27 August).** Net overseas migration is forecast to fall to 225,000 by 2027/28, down from a peak of 538,000 in 2022/23; the proposals, reported in The Australian and confirmed by The PIE, would add a further layer of restriction on top of the 295,000 NPL and $2,500 student visa fee already in place. Vicki Thomson (Go8 CEO): "What is not helpful is treating every provider, every student and every institution as though they are the same." ([The PIE, 27 August](https://thepienews.com/australia-weighs-major-new-restrictions-on-international-students-the-australian-reports/))
