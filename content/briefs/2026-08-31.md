## Top of the brief

- **Clare announces the first round of the Strong Beginnings (Excellence) Fund, awarding $1.5 million across six projects at Melbourne Polytechnic, the University of New England and Charles Sturt University (jointly), QUT, the University of Melbourne, Griffith University, and RMIT University — part of a $7 million over four years (plus $1 million per year ongoing) investment in initial teacher education — alongside new mandatory core content requirements embedding neuroscience, literacy and numeracy instruction, and classroom behaviour management into all teaching degrees (27 August).** Domestic undergraduate teaching applications rose 6.5% for 2026, with a 6.3% increase in offers made. Clare: "One of the best ways to help teachers from day one is to improve the training they receive at university." ([Clare, 27 August](https://jasonclare.com.au/media/new-grants-to-strengthen-teacher-training/))
