## Top of the brief

- **BONARD Education data for 2025 shows ELT student weeks fell 23% across the eight major study destinations and student numbers dropped 10%, with Australia recording the steepest individual decline at 35% in student weeks — the worst result of all eight countries tracked — as English Australia CEO Ian Aird highlighted the "tyranny" of Australia's visa regime and noted the study visa application fee is now ten times that of Canada or Ireland (7 September).** BONARD director Ivana Bartosik warned "2026 would likely bring a third year of overall decline" and that opportunity lay in "matching source market, student segment and program type to where demand is actually holding"; the average length of stay across the eight destinations has fallen from 8.3 weeks in 2022 to 6.5 weeks in 2025, the lowest point in a decade, driven by the contraction of the adult segment in favour of shorter junior courses. Aird, following the report's launch: "They've just got to do so much more with less." ([The PIE, 7 September](https://thepienews.com/worsening-elt-declines-signal-market-restructuring/))
