## Top of the brief
- **The 2025 PISA results, released 8 September, show Australia above the OECD average in all three domains — reading (11th globally), maths (17th), and science (11th) — with Australia moving up one place in reading and maintaining its maths ranking, though falling one place in science while holding science scores; the UK's trajectory — up three places in reading, four in maths, and seven in science — underpins Clare's argument that Australia's phonics reform is "the starting gun" for comparable long-term gains.** Clare, in a 9 September opinion piece, cited two Sydney schools where the share of year 1 students on track with reading jumped from 33% to 70% (Shelley Public School) and 26% to 84% (Briar Road Public School) following this year's national phonics screening check rollout; maths curriculum reform and a school "digital detox" are identified as the next reform priorities. Clare: "The reading wars are over, we know what works. Phonics works." ([Ministers, 8 September](https://ministers.education.gov.au/clare/pisa-results-show-why-reforms-we-are-rolling-out-are-so-important); [Clare opinion piece, 9 September](https://jasonclare.com.au/media/opinion-piece-the-reading-wars-are-over-now-its-time-to-emulate-uk-success/))
