## Top of the brief
- **ILAC Education Group, a 30-year-old Canadian ELT provider operating Toronto and Vancouver campuses, filed for bankruptcy on 18 September — affecting approximately 1,000 international students — the second major ELT provider failure in a week after EC English's closure of 25 schools globally, with Canada's January 2024 student visa cap and restrictions on PPP graduates accessing work permits identified as the proximate causes (19 September).** Australia's ELICOS sub-sector sits 56% below its 2019 baseline — the steepest student-week decline of any major destination in 2025 — and the consecutive provider failures confirm that structural ELT demand contraction is operating independently of, and alongside, Australia's own policy tightening; stakeholders described ILAC's closure as "the end of an era." ([The PIE, 19 September](https://thepienews.com/breaking-ilac-education-group-in-canada-files-for-bankruptcy/))
