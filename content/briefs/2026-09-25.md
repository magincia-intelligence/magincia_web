## Top of the brief

- **VET/ELICOS provider registration freeze — in effect from 19 May.** The 12-month pause on new international student provider registrations in the VET and ELICOS sectors is now operative; it runs to 19 May 2027. Government schools, TAFEs, and Table A universities are exempt. ([The PIE, 18 May](https://thepienews.com/australia-freezes-new-overseas-student-provider-registrations/))
