## Top of the brief

- **First-person student testimony published by The PIE on 25 September documents the financial and academic losses from ILAC's 18 September bankruptcy: José Carlos Chávez (Mexico) lost CAD$14,000 and cancelled a planned Vancouver move; Sebastián Cáceres (Chile-Bolivia), enrolled in a sales and marketing program in Vancouver, lost CAD$13,000; by 22 September, Languages Canada had placed 430 of the 1,362 displaced in-person language students at member schools, though Chávez noted "ILAC as an institution has still provided zero direct communication" with affected students.** Cáceres: "It's like a huge, massive punch in the face." KPMG continues as insolvency manager. ([The PIE, 25 September](https://thepienews.com/a-massive-punch-in-the-face-students-pay-the-price-of-ilacs-sudden-collapse/))
