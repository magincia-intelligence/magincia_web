## Top of the brief

- **VET/ELICOS provider registration freeze — in effect from 19 May.** The 12-month pause on new international student provider registrations in the VET and ELICOS sectors is now operative; it runs to 19 May 2027. Government schools, TAFEs, and Table A universities are exempt. ([The PIE, 18 May](https://thepienews.com/australia-freezes-new-overseas-student-provider-registrations/))

## Funding & system architecture

- The Department of Education has published the exposure draft of the 2026 National Research Infrastructure Roadmap for public consultation, with file reference ESE26/9627 (28 September). ([Department of Education, 28 September](https://www.education.gov.au/national-research-infrastructure/resources/2026-nri-roadmap-exposure-draft))

## Sector data

- **Standing figures:** 2026 NPL 295,000 (+25,000 on 2025); visa refusal rates 69% Nepal, 42% India (early 2026); domestic commencements 413,133 (+4.3% YoY); $50m over-enrolment fund; $50m Structural Adjustment Fund.

## Regulator

- The Australian Skills Quality Authority (ASQA) is responsible for regulating VET and ELICOS providers in Australia.

- **19 May 2027** — ASQA 12-month VET/ELICOS registration pause expires.
