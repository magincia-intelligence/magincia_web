## Top of the brief
- **VET/ELICOS provider registration freeze — in effect from 19 May.** The 12-month pause on new international student provider registrations in the VET and ELICOS sectors is now operative; it runs to 19 May 2027. Government schools, TAFEs, and Table A universities are exempt. ([The PIE, 18 May](https://thepienews.com/australia-freezes-new-overseas-student-provider-registrations/))
- The Department of Education published the 2026 NRI Roadmap Progress Update (file reference ESE26/9251, 29 September), covering key activities underway in developing the National Research Infrastructure Roadmap and engagement opportunities available to stakeholders alongside the exposure draft consultation opened on 28 September. ([Department of Education, 29 September](https://www.education.gov.au/national-research-infrastructure/resources/2026-nri-roadmap-progress-update-september-2026))
