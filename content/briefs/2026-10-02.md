## Top of the brief
- **Julian Hill warned that ASQA has recorded more than 1,300 new Confirmations of Enrolment and over 1,500 brought-forward commencement dates in the Graduate Diploma of Management (Learning) since his 4 September course cancellation decision, and that he is actively considering using Section 97 of the ESOS Act to suspend providers' ability to recruit new students — which would be the first use of those powers.** The GDML closes to new international student enrolments from 5 October; Home Affairs this week sent formal letters of concern to a number of providers; ASQA is building "a strong body of evidence" against providers suspected of undermining the student visa system. Hill: "The message to providers, agents and students is simple: do not try to game this measure." ([Ministers, 1 October](https://ministers.education.gov.au/hill/international-vet-providers-and-students-notice-following-first-use-new-course-cancellation))

## Funding & system architecture
- **Walsh announced the first four large-scale agreements under the $1 billion Building Early Education Fund on 1 October: a new 87-place Goodstart service in Lalor (VIC, $5.9 million), a new 69-place Goodstart service in Trevallyn (TAS, $6.8 million), an expansion of REED's Merredin (WA) centre by 35 places to 71 ($3.3 million), and an expansion of SDN Hamilton St in Bathurst (NSW) by 24 places to 76 ($1.3 million).** Walsh: "Our $1 billion Building Early Education Fund is delivering more early learning centres for more families in more places, particularly in our outer suburbs and regions." ([Ministers, 1 October](https://ministers.education.gov.au/walsh/grants-not-profit-providers-expand-access-early-education))

## International education
- **TEQSA opened consultation on 28 September on a draft Statement of Regulatory Expectations for adopting a definition of Islamophobia, with submissions due 12 October 2026; the regulator aims to publish both the antisemitism and Islamophobia SREs in October, and is separately co-designing with Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander stakeholders a guidance document on racism towards First Nations peoples on a timeline set with those communities.** ([TEQSA, 28 September](https://www.teqsa.gov.au/about-us/news-and-events/latest-news/consultation-opens-draft-sre-adopting-definition-islamophobia))

## Sector data
- **Standing figures:** 2026 NPL 295,000 (+25,000 on 2025); visa refusal rates 69% Nepal, 42% India (early 2026); domestic commencements 413,133 (+4.3% YoY); $50m over-enrolment fund; $50m Structural Adjustment Fund.

## Regulator
- **TEQSA concluded its compliance assessment of the Australian National University on 7 September, publishing the regulatory decision report on the National Register; the public notice provides no further outcome detail beyond that the assessment is concluded.** ANU was subject to a TEQSA voluntary undertaking in connection with governance concerns that included a Coaldrake-chaired Chancellor selection panel. ([TEQSA, 7 September](https://www.teqsa.gov.au/about-us/news-and-events/latest-news/regulatory-decision-concluded))
