## Top of the brief
- **On 2 October, Julian Hill confirmed that the student visa integrity reforms package took full effect, bringing into force the course-change restrictions — limiting onshore international students to upward AQF-level progression only and requiring a new visa application for any provider or course change — and the dependant visa restrictions announced by Burke on 17 September; from today (5 October), the Graduate Diploma of Management (Learning) is closed to new international student enrolments at all CRICOS-registered providers, following Hill's 1 October warning that more than 1,300 new Confirmations of Enrolment and over 1,500 brought-forward commencement dates had been lodged since the 4 September cancellation decision, with Section 97 ESOS Act provider suspension powers still under active consideration for providers suspected of gaming the measures.** ([Ministers, 2 October](https://ministers.education.gov.au/hill/student-visa-integrity-reforms-take-effect-today))

## Funding & system architecture
- **Walsh and South Australian Minister Malinauskas have announced a $12.7 million partnership to deliver an integrated early years hub at Naracoorte, with the Naracoorte Child Day Care Centre expanding to a new location and bringing together additional health, wellbeing, and early education services for families in South Australia's south-east (2 October).** ([Ministers, 2 October](https://ministers.education.gov.au/walsh/expanding-early-education-and-family-services-naracoorte))

## International education
- **The Department of Education has published plain-language factsheets for a National Code of Practice change that temporarily extends the provider transfer restriction period for overseas students (excluding school students) from 6 to 12 months; under the revised National Code, a student enrolled with an initial provider must remain for at least 12 months before transferring, unless the provider agrees or specific exemptions apply (2 October).** ([Department of Education, 2 October — providers](https://www.education.gov.au/esos-framework/resources/extension-overseas-student-transfer-restriction-period-12-months-providers); [students](https://www.education.gov.au/esos-framework/resources/extension-overseas-student-transfer-restriction-period-12-months-students))
