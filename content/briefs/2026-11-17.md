## Top of the brief

- **Minister Jason Clare announced on 16 November that enrolments in fee-free Uni Ready Courses (bridging courses) have jumped 30% — with approximately 29,000 students expected in 2027 — following ATEC's first allocation cycle, backed by $190 million next year as part of a $1 billion, ten-year commitment; University Study Hubs usage is up more than 50% in 2026, with more than 10,000 students accessing hubs in the first half of the year, and more than half studying health or education.** ([Ministers, 16 November](https://ministers.education.gov.au/clare/enrolments-free-bridging-courses-jump-30-cent))
- **Andrew Norton, in analysis published 16 November, finds that ATEC has allocated 620,718 Commonwealth-supported places to public universities in 2027 — 99.4% of the 624,590 total pool — with an Additional Growth Allocation of 36,238 places above the core load; regionally concentrated allocations gave JCU +29%, Charles Darwin +16%, Federation University +16%, and Sunshine Coast +12%; Norton concludes the MDDE program is "unlikely to work as designed" and argues "the simpler remedy is to repeal the caps on enrolments."** Of the 36,238 AGA places, 60% (21,743) convert existing marginally funded places rather than creating genuinely new student seats. ([Norton, 16 November](https://andrewnorton.id.au/2026/11/16/the-2027-atec-allocation-of-commonwealth-supported-places-to-public-universities/))
- **IDP Education's board rejected a third unsolicited takeover bid from Blackstone on 16 November — A$3.00 per share (approximately US$573 million) — characterising it as "opportunistic" and stating it "substantially undervalues" the company; IDP's FY26 revenue was A$795.4 million, down 9%, amid declining student placement volumes across all major markets.** The A$3.00 offer is a material step up from the A$2.50 bid rejected in September. ([The PIE, 16 November](https://thepienews.com/idp-rejects-sweetened-bid-from-private-equity-giant-blackstone/))

## Funding & system architecture

- **Clare opened applications on 16 November for the fourth round of Commonwealth Teaching Scholarships, with up to 1,000 scholarships on offer for 2027 — worth up to $40,000 for undergraduate initial teacher education and $20,000 for postgraduate students — bringing total awards under the five-year program to approximately 3,000 of a planned 5,000; approximately 30% of recipients to date are from regional, rural, or remote Australia.** A four-year teaching service commitment is required for undergraduates; two years for postgraduates. Clare: "When we were elected the number of people studying teaching was going down. Now it's going up." ([Ministers, 16 November](https://jasonclare.com.au/media/40000-commonwealth-teaching-scholarship-applications-open-for-2027/))

## International education

- **The Open Doors 2026 Report, released 16 November, shows total international student enrolments in the US fell 3% — the first annual decline since 2020/21 — with new enrolments down 18% (nearly 50,000 fewer students); postgraduate enrolments fell 13% and master's enrolments fell more than 20%; a fall 2026 snapshot from 809 institutions projects new enrolments down a further 15%.** The US decline reflects F-1 issuance falling 34% in 2025, the Duration of Status rule (effective 15 September), and CPT tightening; 95% of surveyed institutions said students would look elsewhere if OPT were removed. Australia's NPL of 295,000 and visa refusal rates of 69% (Nepal) and 42% (India) limit the degree to which redirected demand can be absorbed domestically. ([The PIE, 16 November](https://thepienews.com/us-total-number-of-international-students-drops-for-first-time-in-five-years/))
