## Top of the brief

- **UKVI data obtained by The PIE (18 November) show that of 11,415 credibility interviews conducted between May and October 2026, 9,043 — 79% — resulted in a non-credibility finding; rejection rates after interview were highest for Nepal (92%) and India (89%), followed by Nigeria (84%) and Bangladesh (79%); reasons included limited program knowledge, weak evidence of genuine study intent, and doubts about funds.** For Australia, its own visa refusal rates — 69% for Nepal and 42% for India in early 2026 — remain the binding constraint on absorbing redirected demand from applicants turned away from the UK. Home Office official Oliver Rae told UUKi's International Student Recruitment Conference there is no cap on student numbers: "our focus is on genuine students." ([The PIE, 18 November](https://thepienews.com/ukvi-rejects-80-of-students-called-to-interview-as-visa-refusals-surge/))

## International education

- **The 2027 QS Asia University Rankings (released 3 November, reported by The PIE on 17 November) placed the University of Hong Kong first, with Hong Kong taking three of Asia's top five positions; Singapore's two flagship universities dropped out of the top five; 16 Malaysian institutions reached their highest-ever rankings; and India's representation in the top 200 rose from 20 to 21 universities, though all six of its highest-ranked institutions lost ground.** ([The PIE, 17 November](https://thepienews.com/hong-kong-takes-three-of-asias-top-five-places-in-qs-university-rankings/))

- **Irish universities face an estimated €8 million fee shortfall following a surge in student-visa refusals, with at least one institution warning of cuts to master's programmes; Ireland's Labour Party has called for a government review while the Department of Justice maintains its application criteria are unchanged (17 November).** Ireland joins the UK and US as a competitor destination experiencing visa-barrier escalation in the same South Asian and African source markets Australia also recruits from. ([The PIE, 17 November](https://thepienews.com/irish-universities-face-e8m-fee-shortfall-after-student-visa-refusals/))
