## Top of the brief
- **Luke Sheehy, CEO of Universities Australia, told a policy roundtable co-hosted with the Centre for the Study of Higher Education on 19 November that Job-Ready Graduates cut Commonwealth funding per place by 15% and removed roughly $800 million a year from the system while failing to change student course choices, which remained driven by interest and aptitude rather than price; Sheehy called for a credible replacement addressing three questions — the true cost of quality teaching, how costs should be shared between government and students, and how HECS-HELP should work after graduation — framing reform as a task requiring political durability well before the 2028 federal election, and noting that ATEC's consultation on its costing and pricing framework closes 7 December 2026.** Sheehy: "There's an important difference between an efficient system and an underfunded one." ([Universities Australia, 19 November](https://universitiesaustralia.edu.au/speeches/opening-remarks-university-funding-beyond-jrg-policy-roundtable/))
